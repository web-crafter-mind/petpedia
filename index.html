When to See a Vet

Critical health warning signs for different pets.

Dogs

  • • Excessive panting/lethargy
  • • Changes in appetite/water intake
  • • Difficulty standing or walking
  • • Red or cloudy eyes

Cats

  • • Hiding more than usual
  • • Sudden aggression
  • • Rapid weight loss/gain
  • • Trouble using the litter box

Birds

  • • Fluffed up feathers for hours
  • • Change in dropping color
  • • Sitting on bottom of cage
  • • Tail bobbing while breathing

Fish

  • • Gasping at surface
  • • Clamped fins against body
  • • White spots or fuzzy patches
  • • Erratic swimming patterns
